Questions

The things people ask before buying.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Every section, colour, font and piece of text is changed in Shopify's drag-and-drop theme editor. Upload the theme, click Customize and edit what you see.

Will the 3D slow my store down?

The 3D scene loads only after the rest of your page has loaded, pauses when it scrolls out of view and switches off for visitors who use data saver or reduced motion. Your text, products and buttons appear first.

Will it work on my Shopify plan?

Yes. These are standard Online Store 2.0 themes and work on every Shopify plan, with your existing products, collections and apps.

Can I use one theme now and switch later?

Yes. Install as many of the five as you like and keep them in your theme library. Your products stay the same whichever theme is live.

What do I get with a custom theme?

After checkout you get a short brief to fill in: your brand, logo, colours, products and customers. We design and build a theme around it, confirm your delivery date within 2 working days of receiving the brief, and include two rounds of changes. You get an install-ready theme file and a setup guide.

Do the demos show real products?

The demos use a sample catalogue with illustrated products so you can try every feature. Once installed, the theme shows your own products and photos.

Can I use the themes on client or multiple stores?

You can use them on any store you own or run. Reselling or sharing the theme files themselves isn't allowed.
